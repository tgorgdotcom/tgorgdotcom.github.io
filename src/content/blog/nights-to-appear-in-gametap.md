I just stumbled upon the following statement in the "NiGHTS: Journey of Dreams" website;
> Also, make sure to check out GameTap to play an emulated version of the original NiGHTS Into Dreams on your computer.

You can check it out for yourselves here: <http://www.sega.com/gamesite/nights/>. Go to Story > Behind the Story to look at the text! This is going to be awesome...

I am a big fan of classic games, so it's pretty exciting seeing NiGHTS coming to GameTap. I remember playing Christmas NiGHTS, a Christmas-themed demo version of NiGHTS, and getting all the "presents", unlockables that were accessible after accomplishing a certain task, except one. I did manage to get "Sonic into Dreams" though...

**MORE UPDATE:** Sega's Japanese [Sonic Team web site has announced](http://translate.google.com/translate?hl=en&u=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.sonicteam.com%2F) the original NiGHTS to be released for the PS2 on February 21, 2008 (it's a small announcement -- you'll see it on the right). Just thought I'd bring that up. Not sure if this is Japan only or not though...

**UPDATE:** Sega's taken down the "Behind the Story" section... Interesting...
