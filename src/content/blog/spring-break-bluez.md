Hey peoples. I'm **back at home** for the week until March 11th, so if you'd like to visit, let me know.

It's kinda interesting to me how spring break is supposed to be the giant party that everyone's invited to, yet everyone seems to come uninvited. I really don't see any point in celebrating it the way most college students do. Heck, I don't even know what were celebrating in the first place! And, apparently, celebrating means getting drunk, passing out, and when you've awaken, you find out your either on the cold floor of a room in a cheesy motel, or in the cheesy motel's mens restroom, which is kinda awkward considering that it has a dirt floor.

In all seriousness, spring break is nice for a good excuse to take a vacation, but I don't have the money do anything that would be otherwise considered a "vacation". So I'll be doing something practical: working. Otherwise my spring break would consist of laying around trying to figure out what to do during the agonizing 7 days I have at home.

Apparently, Easter break will be much cooler.
