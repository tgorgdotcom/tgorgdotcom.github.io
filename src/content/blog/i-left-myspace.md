Hey guys. As most of you know, I left the MySpace scene. Basically, the ads posted on MySpace weren't the greatest in terms of content (girls in bikinis, etc.), and for me, it was a big deal.

Otherwise MySpace is a really cool service. It's a great way to connect with friends; even friends a person has never seen in awhile. It's interesting to note that it has now been bought by News Corp. (the people who own FOX), which also reminds me how people are getting confused with News Corp. and Fox News. News Corp. owns Fox News, but is not Fox News.

It's also interesting to point out that News Corp. owns Zondervan, which produces Christian books and Bibles (specifically the NIV).

Okay, this post just became meaningless... I know too much about companies...
